# Department Hub

Please help me build a page for below requirement:
📄 Internal Department Management System Requirements

(Onboarding, Learning & Internal Operations)

1. Overview

The system is designed for internal department use only to manage staff onboarding, training, and day-to-day employee activities within the department. It will centralize processes, improve coordination, and reduce manual tasks.

2. Objectives

Standardize onboarding within the department

Track employee learning and skill development

Improve visibility of staff progress and tasks

Simplify internal communication and coordination

Maintain organized employee records

3. Scope

This system is limited to:

Department staff and managers

Internal onboarding processes

Department-level training and knowledge sharing

4. Key Features

4.1 Onboarding Management

Purpose: Ensure new staff are properly onboarded within the department

Features:

Create new staff profile (basic info, role, start date)

Department-specific onboarding checklist

Task assignment (e.g., manager, buddy, IT support)

Track onboarding progress

Upload required documents (internal use)

Status dashboard (Pending / In Progress / Completed)

4.2 Learning & Knowledge Management

Purpose: Manage internal training and knowledge sharing

Features:

Upload training materials (documents, videos, links)

Assign training to staff

Track completion status

Department-specific learning paths

Knowledge base (guidelines, SOPs, best practices)

Simple assessment or acknowledgment (optional)

4.3 Staff Management (Department Level)

Features:

Staff directory (within department)

Roles and responsibilities tracking

Reporting structure (manager, team lead)

Basic employee status (active, probation, etc.)

4.4 Task & Activity Tracking

Features:

Assign internal tasks to staff

Track task status (To Do / In Progress / Done)

Deadline reminders

Manager visibility of team workload

4.5 Communication & Notifications

Features:

Internal announcements

Notifications for onboarding tasks and training

Reminders for pending actions

5. User Roles

Department Admin / Manager

Manage onboarding, assign training and tasks, monitor progress

Staff

Complete onboarding tasks, access training, update task status

6. Functional Requirements

Secure login (internal users only)

Role-based access (manager vs staff)

Simple dashboard for each user

Tracking and reporting (onboarding, training, tasks)

Search and filter for staff and records

7. Non-Functional Requirements

User-friendly interface

Fast response time

Secure internal data access

Accessible via web browser

Scalable for department growth

8. Reporting

Onboarding completion status

Training progress reports

Task completion tracking

Staff activity overview

9. Success Criteria

Faster onboarding within the department

Higher training completion rate

Improved team coordination

Reduced manual tracking (Excel, emails, etc.)
